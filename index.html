Reservation

RESERVATIONS Lunch is served from Tuesday to Friday between 11:30 and 15:00.
While walk-ins are always welcome, we recommend making a reservation to ensure availability.
Dinner is served Tuesday to Friday, 18:00–23:00 and on Saturdays from 17:00–23:00.
To help us reduce food waste and ensure the best possible experience for all our guests, we kindly ask for a
100 € deposit per guest when booking a dinner table. This allows us to plan ahead and serve you better.
This amount will be fully deducted from your final bill at the restaurant.
If you have any allergies or dietary restrictions, please let us know in advance so our kitchen can be well prepared.
Please note that we are unable to offer dairy-free or vegan menus.
For groups larger than six, please contact us directly via email to make a reservation:mh2473419@gmail.com

About Us

For decades, our restaurant has stood as a cornerstone of the community, a place where tradition meets culinary excellence. Known for our unwavering commitment to quality and service, we have spent years perfecting an atmosphere that feels both timeless and inviting. Our meticulously designed interiors and warm, thoughtful lighting create the perfect backdrop for everything from intimate family gatherings to grand celebrations. Our menu is a curated journey through flavor, offering a masterful blend of traditional national dishes, authentic Chinese specialties, and perfectly prepared fried favorites that have kept guests returning for generations. Every plate we serve is a testament to our passion for fresh ingredients and time-honored recipes. We believe that dining is more than just a meal; it is an experience defined by hospitality and attention to detail. As we bring our legacy into the digital age, our mission remains the same: to provide an unforgettable setting where the food is exceptional and every guest feels like family. We invite you to explore our story, browse our signature dishes, and discover why we remain the city’s most cherished dining destination. Thank you for being a part of our journey; we look forward to welcoming you back to the table.
